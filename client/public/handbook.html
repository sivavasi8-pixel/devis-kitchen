Devi's Kitchen — User Manual

Everything you can do in the app, organized by role. Pick yours below.

👑 Owner guide

Full run of the place: dashboard, POS, orders, inventory, staff, the menu, and reports. Everything staff can do, plus menu editing, reports, and roster management.

  Log in at /login with your owner email and password.

Dashboard

Your landing page after login — a snapshot of today.

  • Four stat cards: today's revenue, orders today (with a pending count), staff currently on shift, and low-stock item count.
  • Order queue: the most recent orders. Advance a kitchen-stage order (placed → preparing → ready) right from the dropdown next to it, or click Mark paid for a cash order once it's settled.
  • Each order links to its printable receipt.
  • Low stock panel: anything at or below its reorder level, so you can act before you run out.
The status dropdown here only offers the kitchen steps. Delivery hand-off (out for delivery / picked up / delivered) happens on the Orders page or the rider's own screen.

POS — ring up a walk-in sale

For customers ordering at the counter — pickup or dine-in only (no delivery from here).

  1. Pick a category tab, then tap items to add them to the sale.
  2. Choose Pickup or Dine-in (dine-in asks for a table number).
  3. Optionally type the customer's name — defaults to "Walk-in".
  4. Pick a payment method (Cash / UPI / Card).
  5. Click Charge and ring up — this creates the order and deducts ingredient stock automatically.
  6. Print the receipt, or start the next sale.

You can also toggle an item sold out / available directly from the POS grid — handy when the kitchen runs out mid-shift.

Made-to-order items (like "Biryani by the kg") and Today's Specials don't show up in POS — those are ordered by customers through the online Order page's own forms.

Orders

Every order the shop has ever taken, searchable and filterable.

  • Search by customer name or order number; filter by status.
  • Advance status per order: placed → preparing → ready → (out for delivery / picked up) → delivered. Delivery and pickup orders both flow through this same dropdown.
  • Assign a rider — appears once a delivery or pickup order hits "ready".
  • Mark paid for anything still unpaid (cash orders stay unpaid until you or staff confirm payment).
  • Cancel an order — automatically restocks any ingredients that had been deducted for it.
  • Every row links to a printable receipt.

Inventory

Raw ingredient stock — what the kitchen actually consumes, separate from the menu itself.

  • Stat cards show total items tracked, how many are low, and how many are fully out.
  • The list sorts out-of-stock and low-stock items to the top automatically.
  • Anyone can quick-set a new quantity on any ingredient (type a number, click Set) — useful after a delivery arrives.
  • Owner-only: Add, Edit, or Delete an ingredient (name, unit, starting quantity, reorder level, supplier). Deleting one also removes it from any recipe that used it.
Stock deducts automatically when an order is placed, based on each menu item's recipe (see Menu below) — you don't need to manually subtract anything for a normal sale.

Staff

Your roster, shift status, and task board.

  • Stat cards: on shift now, open tasks, absent today.
  • Anyone can change a team member's status (clocked in / on break / absent / clocked out) from the dropdown.
  • Owner-only: edit someone's shift text, remove a person from the roster (this also revokes their login if they had one), and add new staff.
  • Adding a staff member: name, role, shift, and a checkbox for "this person is a delivery rider". Optionally also check "grant app access" to give them an email + password so they can log in themselves (this creates their account with the matching role — rider if the rider box is checked, staff otherwise).
  • Task assignments: add a task, assign it to a specific person, give it a due time, tick it off (○ → ✓) when done, or edit/delete it later.

Menu Owner only

What customers see on the Order page — the one page staff can't access.

  • Categories: Mains, Breads, Starters, Desserts, Beverages, and Custom are built in; add your own with + New category in the item form — it appears as a new tab here and on the customer Order page automatically.
  • Adding/editing an item: name, category, price, unit (plate, piece, kg…), description, and a cover photo.
  • Leave price blank for a made-to-order item — use category custom for these (e.g. "Biryani by the kg", priced per kg). Customers order these from a dedicated form instead of tapping "Add".
  • ⭐ Special toggle: features an item in "Today's Specials" on the Order page until midnight, then it clears itself automatically — no need to remember to turn it off.
  • 🔥 Popular toggle: shows a "Popular" badge on the item's product card.
  • In stock / Sold out toggle: hides the "Add" button from customers without deleting the item.
  • Recipe editor (per item): the bill of materials — how much of each inventory ingredient one unit of this item uses. This is what drives automatic stock deduction on every order.
  • Photos (per item): up to 4 extra gallery photos beyond the cover image, shown as a swipeable carousel to customers.
  • Switch between list and card view with the toggle in the top-right.
The dropdown also offers a dedicated ⭐ Today's special category, for an item that should never appear in the regular Mains/Breads/etc. tabs. But that category alone doesn't show it to customers — you still need to flip the ⭐ Special toggle on it too, or it won't appear anywhere until you do.

Reports Owner only

Revenue, expenses, and what's selling.

  • Stat cards: all-time revenue, expenses, profit, total orders, and a placed/delivered breakdown.
  • 7-day revenue chart — hover a bar for that day's revenue and order count.
  • Best sellers — top items by quantity sold.
  • Expenses: log one (description, amount, category — ingredients/utilities/rent/wages/other) and remove any later. This week's spend and profit are called out above the list.
  • Export CSV — downloads the week's revenue, best sellers, and expenses as a spreadsheet.
  • Print / Save as PDF — uses your browser's print dialog; the nav is hidden automatically for a clean printout.

Notifications

The bell icon (sidebar on desktop, top bar on mobile) checks for alerts every 30 seconds — things like low stock or orders needing attention — with a red badge showing how many are waiting. Click it to see the list.

👩‍🍳 Staff guide

The day-to-day running of the kitchen and counter: dashboard, POS, orders, inventory, and the shared staff/task board. Menu editing and Reports are owner-only and won't appear in your nav.

  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you.

Dashboard

Your landing page — today's revenue, order count, who's on shift, and low-stock items, plus the live order queue where you can advance an order (placed → preparing → ready) or mark a cash order paid.

POS — ring up a walk-in sale

For counter customers — pickup or dine-in.

  1. Tap items by category to build the sale.
  2. Choose Pickup or Dine-in (with table number).
  3. Optional customer name, pick a payment method, then Charge and ring up.
  4. Print the receipt or start the next sale.

You can also mark an item sold out / available right from the grid.

Orders

  • Search or filter every order by status.
  • Advance an order's status through its full lifecycle, including delivery hand-off steps.
  • Assign a rider once an order is "ready".
  • Mark an order paid, or cancel it (stock gets restocked automatically).

Inventory

View stock levels and quick-set a new quantity for any ingredient (type a number next to it, click Set) — e.g. after a delivery comes in. Adding, editing, or deleting an ingredient definition itself is owner-only.

Staff & tasks

You can see the full roster and change anyone's shift status (clocked in / on break / absent / clocked out) — handy for clocking yourself or a coworker in and out.

Task board: tick tasks done, edit them, or add a new one and assign it to a teammate.

Adding/removing staff, editing shift schedules, and granting logins are owner-only actions.

Notifications

The bell icon polls for alerts (like low stock) every 30 seconds — click it to see what needs attention.

🛵 Rider guide

A single, focused screen: the deliveries assigned to you, and a pool of ready orders you can pick up yourself.

  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you.

My Deliveries

This is the only page you'll see — it refreshes itself automatically every 15 seconds.

Assigned to me — orders someone (or you) has already put on your plate:

  • Each shows the customer, items, delivery address & phone (for delivery orders), and payment status.
  • When an order is ready, tap Mark picked up — this moves a delivery order to "out for delivery", or a pickup order straight to "picked up".
  • Once you've dropped it off, tap Mark delivered.

Ready for pickup — unassigned — orders that are ready but nobody's claimed yet:

  • Tap Claim this order to assign it to yourself. It then moves into your "Assigned to me" list.

Every order links to a printable receipt if you need to double-check the details.

🛍️ Customer guide

Browse the menu, order online, and track what you've ordered — no account needed to look around, but you'll need one to check out.

  New here? Create an account — just a name, email, and password. Already have one? Log in.

Order online

The main menu page — open to everyone, logged in or not.

  • Search the whole menu by name, or browse by category chip (Mains, Breads, Starters, Desserts, Beverages, Custom order, and any others the kitchen has added).
  • Today's Specials — a highlighted strip at the top, when the kitchen has anything featured that day. These clear automatically at midnight.
  • Tap the ♥ on any item to favorite it (saved on your device, not tied to your account).
  • Some items have a photo carousel — swipe or use the arrows to see extra photos.
  • Custom order tab: for made-to-order items priced by weight (like "Biryani by the kg"). Enter the quantity in kg, optionally a protein/style, spice level, and a needed-by date — the price calculates automatically as you type.
  • Your order panel (right side / bottom sheet on mobile): shows everything in your cart with any notes, and the running total.

Checking out

  1. Add items to your cart from the menu (or the Custom order form).
  2. Choose how you're getting it: Delivery (needs an address, phone optional), Pickup, or Dine-in (needs a table number).
  3. Pick a payment method: Cash, UPI, or Card.
  4. Click Checkout.

If you're not logged in yet, your cart stays exactly as it is — just log in or create an account, and you'll come right back to checkout.

UPI and Card orders are treated as paid immediately. Cash orders stay "unpaid" in the system until the kitchen or your rider confirms payment when the order arrives.

Once placed, you'll see an order confirmation with a link to your printable receipt, and a live tracker banner will follow your active order around the site until it's delivered.

My orders

Your full order history, newest first — item list, channel, total, and live status for each.

  • Click receipt on any order to view or print it.
  • You can cancel an order yourself, but only while it's still just "placed" — once the kitchen starts preparing it, you'll need to call the restaurant to cancel.

  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you. You'll land straight on My Deliveries, the only page you need. You can also reach it from My deliveries in the top bar if you open the customer menu.

My Deliveries

This page refreshes itself automatically every 15 seconds, so you don't need to reload to see new orders.

Assigned to me: orders that are yours. Each one shows the customer, items, address, a tap-to-call phone number and a Navigate link that opens the exact drop-off point in Google Maps (when the address has a location). It also shows how far the drop-off is from the restaurant, and payment status.

  • A flame means the restaurant flagged the order urgent. Urgent orders always sit at the top.
  • "Booked for…" means the customer chose a delivery time. Aim for it.
  • "Collect ₹…" in red means the customer hasn't paid. Take that amount (cash, or as agreed) at the door.
  • "N other orders within 1 km" means other waiting orders are close to this one, so it may be worth taking them together.
1

When an order is ready, collect it and tap Mark picked up. This moves a delivery order to "out for delivery", or a pickup order straight to "picked up".

2

Once you've dropped it off, tap Mark delivered.

Changed your mind about an order you claimed? While it's still waiting at the restaurant (not yet picked up), tap Release and it goes back to the unassigned list for another rider.

Ready for pickup, unassigned: orders that are ready but nobody has claimed. Urgent ones come first, then the nearest. Tap Claim this order to take it.

If two riders tap Claim at the same moment, only the first gets it. The other sees "Someone already picked this one up" and the list refreshes. Every order links to a printable receipt if you need to double-check the details.

My account

Open My account in the sidebar (or the ☰ menu on mobile) to change your own password.

  1. Enter your current password.
  2. Enter a new one, at least 8 characters, twice.
  3. Tap Update password.

A publicly known password such as the sample ones can't be used. If you forget it completely, ask your owner.

Dine-in orders never show up here. Only delivery and pickup orders need a rider. You can only move your own orders forward, and only to picked up, out for delivery or delivered.

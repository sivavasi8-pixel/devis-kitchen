A single, focused screen: the deliveries assigned to you, and orders you can pick up yourself.

← Back to the app
  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you. You'll land straight on My Deliveries — it's the only page you need.

My Deliveries

This page refreshes itself automatically every 15 seconds, so you don't need to manually reload to see new orders come in.

Assigned to me — orders someone (or you) has put on your plate. Each one shows the customer, items, delivery address & phone (for delivery orders), and payment status.

1

When an order is marked ready by the kitchen, tap Mark picked up — this moves a delivery order to "out for delivery", or a pickup order straight to "picked up".

2

Once you've dropped it off (or the customer's collected it), tap Mark delivered.

Ready for pickup — unassigned — orders that are ready but nobody's claimed yet. Tap Claim this order to assign it to yourself; it then moves into your "Assigned to me" list.

Every order links to a printable receipt if you need to double-check the details — items, address, or payment method.

Dine-in orders never show up here — only delivery and pickup orders need a rider.

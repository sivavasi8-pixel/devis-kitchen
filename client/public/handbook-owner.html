Owner Manual

Everything you can do at Devi's Kitchen as the owner — full run of the place.

← Back to the app
  Log in at /login with your owner email and password and you'll land on the Dashboard. You get everything staff can do, plus Menu editing, Reports, Settings and full roster management. The restaurant's web address itself (/) shows the customer menu. Use Shop console in the top bar, or View storefront in the sidebar, to switch between the two.

Dashboard

Your landing page: a snapshot of today. Use New sale (top right) to jump to the counter sale screen.

  • Four stat cards: revenue today (with the order count), to cook now (orders waiting or being prepared), awaiting payment (how many orders and how much money still needs confirming), and running low (ingredients at or below their reorder level).
  • Order queue: everything still in progress, oldest first. Each row has a one-tap button for the next step (Start preparing → Mark ready → Out for delivery / Collected / Served → Delivered) and Mark paid. Each order links to its receipt.
  • Running low: each low ingredient with a bar showing how close it is to running out.
  • On shift: who is clocked in, with their role and shift.
  • Last 7 days revenue chart (owner only), linking to Reports.

Counter sale (POS)

For customers ordering at the counter: pickup or dine-in (no delivery from here).

  1. Pick All items or a category tab, then tap an item to add it. Tap again to add more. Use the − / + buttons in the sale to change a quantity.
  2. Choose Pickup or Dine-in (dine-in asks for a table number).
  3. Optionally type the customer's name. It defaults to "Walk-in".
  4. Pick a payment method (Cash / UPI / Card).
  5. Click the big Charge button. This creates a paid order and deducts ingredient stock automatically.
  6. Print the receipt, or just start the next sale. Clear empties the sale.

Use Mark sold out / Mark available under any tile when the kitchen runs out mid-shift.

Made-to-order items (like "Biryani by the kg") don't show up here. Customers order those through the online Order page's own form. Prices always come from the menu, never from the screen.

Orders

Two views, switched at the top right.

  • Board (default): columns for New, Preparing, Ready, Out for delivery and Done today. Each card has one button to move it to the next step. It refreshes by itself every 30 seconds, so new online orders appear without reloading.
  • All orders: the full history as a table, with a status filter and a dropdown to correct a status.
  • Search by customer name, order number or phone, and filter by All / Delivery / Pickup / Dine-in.
  • Booked time: orders a customer booked for later show the day and time on the card. Orders without one are "as soon as possible".
  • Assign a rider once a delivery or pickup order is Ready. A rider can also claim it from their own screen.
  • Mark urgent on a delivery order moves it to the top of every rider's list.
  • Payment: a customer who paid by UPI can upload a screenshot. When one is there, View receipt opens it so you can check the money arrived, then Mark paid. Customer orders always start unpaid.
  • Cancel order asks you to confirm, then restocks any ingredients that had been deducted.
  • Orders created from a meal subscription carry a small subscription badge.
A cancelled order can't be reopened. If the customer changes their mind, place a new order instead. This keeps stock correct.

Customers

Everyone who has signed up, with their email and phone.

  • A highlighted row means the customer asked for a PIN reset because they can't remember their PIN. Set a new 6-digit PIN for them and let them know.
  • Reset password sets a new password (at least 8 characters) for a customer who is locked out.
  • Customers who signed up before PINs existed show "Not set" in the PIN column. Setting one lets them use the forgot-password page.

Inventory

Raw ingredient stock: what the kitchen actually consumes, separate from the menu itself.

  • Stat cards show how many items are tracked, how many are low and how many are out. Out-of-stock and low-stock items sort to the top.
  • Anyone can quick-set a new quantity (type a number, click Set), for example after a delivery arrives.
  • Used this week / this month shows how much of each ingredient orders have consumed.
  • View history lists every change to that ingredient: which order used it, a cancelled order giving it back, or someone setting it by hand.
  • Create reorder task (on low items) adds a task to the Staff page so someone remembers to buy it.
  • Owner only: Add, Edit or Delete an ingredient: name, unit, quantity, reorder level, supplier and cost per unit. Deleting one also removes it from any recipe that used it.
Stock deducts automatically when an order is placed, based on each menu item's recipe (see Menu), and comes back automatically if the order is cancelled.

Staff

Your roster, shift status and task board.

  • Stat cards: on shift now, open tasks, absent today.
  • Each person can change their own status (clocked in / on break / absent / clocked out). Only the owner can change someone else's.
  • Owner only: edit someone's shift text, remove a person from the roster (this also revokes their login), and add new staff.
  • Adding a staff member: name, role, shift, and a checkbox for "this person is a delivery rider". Optionally give them app access with an email and a password of at least 8 characters. That creates their login with the matching role (rider if the rider box is ticked, staff otherwise). An email that's already in use is refused.
  • Task assignments: add a task, assign it to someone, give it a due time, tick it off when done, or edit or delete it later.

Subscribers & meal plans

Open Subscribers in the sidebar. Customers subscribe to a plan, and the kitchen is fed automatically from it.

  • Subscribers tab: three stat cards (active subscriptions, recurring revenue per cycle, total ever subscribed), then every subscriber with their plan, what they picked for each meal, delivery details, amount and whether it's paid. Buttons: Mark paid, Pause, Resume, Cancel (asks you to confirm).
  • Plans tab (owner only):
    • Discount per cycle length: a % off for Daily, Weekly and Monthly plans. Changing it only affects future subscriptions, never one already paid for.
    • Delivery fee: a flat charge per day for delivery subscriptions, never discounted. Pickup plans pay none.
    • Plans: switch a plan on/off, or delete it. A plan that has ever had a subscriber can't be deleted, so switch it to inactive instead to stop new signups.
    • Add a plan: a name, the length (Daily / Weekly / Monthly), which meals it covers and the most items per meal. You don't set a price: it's worked out from the dishes the customer picks.

What happens every day. Once an hour the system checks every active subscription and creates that day's orders from what each person picked. They land on the Orders board with a small subscription badge, take ingredients out of stock like any order, and you and your staff get a notification. A paused or cancelled plan produces no orders.

Renewal. When a cycle runs out (Daily 1 day, Weekly 7, Monthly 30) the plan rolls into the next cycle with prices and discount recalculated, and the new cycle is marked unpaid until you press Mark paid. The daily orders copy the subscription's payment status.

Staff can see the subscriber list and mark paid, pause, resume or cancel. Only the owner sees the Plans tab.

Menu Owner only

What customers see on the Order page, and the one page staff can't access.

  • Categories: Mains, Breads, Starters, Desserts, Beverages and Custom are built in. Add your own with + New category in the item form and it appears here and on the customer Order page automatically.
  • Adding/editing an item: name, category, price, unit (plate, piece, kg…), prep time, description and a cover photo. Negative or non-numeric prices are rejected.
  • Remove photo: while editing an item, click Remove photo (with Undo) and save to clear its cover photo. Choosing a new photo replaces it.
  • Leave price blank for a made-to-order item. Use category custom for per-kg items (e.g. "Biryani by the kg", priced per kg). Customers order these from a dedicated form instead of tapping "Add".
  • ⭐ Special toggle: features an item in "Today's Specials" until midnight, then clears itself.
  • 🔥 Popular toggle: shows a "Popular" badge on the item.
  • In stock / Sold out toggle: hides the "Add" button without deleting the item.
  • Prep time (minutes): blank means "Ready now"; a number shows "~N min" next to the price.
  • Recipe editor (per item): how much of each inventory ingredient one unit of the item uses. This drives automatic stock deduction. For per-kg items, the recipe is per kg.
  • Photos (per item): up to 4 extra gallery photos, shown as a swipeable carousel.
  • Switch between list and card view with the toggle at the top right.
The dropdown also offers a dedicated ⭐ Today's special category for an item that should never appear in the regular tabs. That category alone doesn't show it to customers: you still need to flip the ⭐ Special toggle on it too.

Reports Owner only

Revenue, expenses and what's selling. Cancelled orders are left out of revenue and best sellers, and days follow India time.

  • Stat cards: all-time revenue, expenses, profit, total orders and a status breakdown.
  • 7-day revenue chart and best sellers.
  • Ingredient cost (estimated): what the ingredients consumed by real orders cost over the last 7 and 30 days, using the cost per unit you set on the Inventory page. Ingredients with no cost on file are left out, and it's separate from the expenses you log by hand.
  • Expenses: log one (description, amount, category) and remove any later. This week's spend and profit are shown above the list.
  • Export CSV and Print / Save as PDF for the week.

Settings Owner only

Shop-wide details you can change any time, without a developer.

  • Ordering hours & delivery:
    • Opens / Closes and the days you're closed. "As soon as possible" orders are only accepted while open.
    • Slot length (15 to 60 minutes), how many days ahead customers can book, and the minimum notice for a same-day slot.
    • Delivery zones: list them nearest first. Each has a distance (km from the restaurant), a fee, a "free above" order amount (0 means the fee always applies) and a minimum order. An order goes in the first zone that covers its distance, and anything beyond the last zone isn't delivered. With no zones, delivery is free with no limit.
  • Shop location: the restaurant's address and exact pin. Use Use my current location while you're at the restaurant, Pick on map, or paste a Google Maps link. Delivery distance, fees and "km from restaurant" for riders all depend on this, so set it first.
  • Payment · UPI: your UPI ID and the name shown to payers. Once set, customers with unpaid UPI/Card orders get a QR code and can upload a payment screenshot.
Starting values are 11:00 to 22:00, 30-minute slots, 3 days ahead, and two sample zones. Check them against how you really work before going live.

Notifications & account safety

The bell icon (sidebar on desktop, top bar on mobile) checks for alerts every 30 seconds, like low stock or orders needing attention, with a red badge. Turn on push notifications from the sidebar to get new-order alerts on your device even when the app isn't open.

Logging out asks you to confirm. Too many wrong login attempts pause logging in for about 15 minutes.

Change your own password any time from My account in the sidebar (current password, then a new one of at least 8 characters). If you forget it completely, your developer can reset it with npm run set-password -- email new-password (run in the server folder). Customers change theirs from their own My account page, and you can reset a customer's from Customers.

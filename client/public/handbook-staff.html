Staff Manual

The day-to-day running of the kitchen and counter at Devi's Kitchen.

← Back to the app
  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you. You get the dashboard, POS, orders, inventory, and the staff/task board — Menu editing and Reports are owner-only and won't appear in your nav.

Dashboard

Your landing page — today's revenue, order count, who's on shift, and low-stock items, plus the live order queue where you can advance an order (placed → preparing → ready) or mark a cash order paid.

POS — ring up a walk-in sale

For counter customers — pickup or dine-in.

  1. Tap items by category to build the sale.
  2. Choose Pickup or Dine-in (with table number).
  3. Optional customer name, pick a payment method, then Charge and ring up.
  4. Print the receipt or start the next sale.

You can also mark an item sold out / available right from the grid.

Orders

  • Search or filter every order by status.
  • Advance an order's status through its full lifecycle, including delivery hand-off steps.
  • Assign a rider once an order is "ready".
  • Mark an order paid, or cancel it (stock gets restocked automatically).

Inventory

View stock levels and quick-set a new quantity for any ingredient (type a number next to it, click Set) — e.g. after a delivery comes in. Adding, editing, or deleting an ingredient definition itself is owner-only.

Staff & tasks

You can see the full roster and change anyone's shift status (clocked in / on break / absent / clocked out) — handy for clocking yourself or a coworker in and out.

Task board: tick tasks done, edit them, or add a new one and assign it to a teammate.

Adding/removing staff, editing shift schedules, and granting logins are owner-only actions.

Notifications

The bell icon polls for alerts (like low stock) every 30 seconds — click it to see what needs attention.

Need something you don't see here — editing the menu, viewing reports, adding a teammate? That's your owner's job. Ask them.

Staff Manual

The day-to-day running of the kitchen and counter at Devi's Kitchen.

← Back to the app
  Log in at /login with the email and password your owner set up for you. You get the dashboard, counter sale, orders, inventory, customers and the staff/task board. Menu, Reports and Settings are owner-only and won't appear in your nav. The restaurant's web address itself shows the customer menu: use Shop console or View storefront to switch.

Dashboard

Your landing page: today's revenue, what's waiting to be cooked, how much money still needs confirming, and what's running low. The order queue shows everything in progress, oldest first. Each row has a one-tap button for its next step and a Mark paid button. Below it you'll see who's on shift.

Counter sale (POS)

For counter customers: pickup or dine-in.

  1. Tap items to add them (All items or by category). Use − / + to change quantities.
  2. Choose Pickup or Dine-in (with a table number).
  3. Optional customer name, pick a payment method, then tap Charge.
  4. Print the receipt or start the next sale.

You can also mark an item sold out / available from its tile.

Orders

  • Board view: columns New, Preparing, Ready, Out for delivery and Done today, with one button per card to move it forward. All orders shows the full history.
  • Search by name, order number or phone, and filter by Delivery, Pickup or Dine-in.
  • Booked-for-later orders show their day and time. Everything else is "as soon as possible".
  • Assign a rider once an order is Ready, and Mark urgent on a delivery that can't wait.
  • Customer orders start unpaid. If a customer uploaded a payment screenshot, View receipt opens it. Check the money arrived, then Mark paid.
  • Cancel order asks you to confirm and restocks the ingredients automatically. A cancelled order can't be reopened.

Customers

Look up a customer's contact details. If someone is locked out, a highlighted row means they asked for a PIN reset: set a new 6-digit PIN. You can also set a new password (8+ characters) for them.

Inventory

View stock levels and quick-set a new quantity for any ingredient (type a number next to it, click Set), for example after a delivery comes in. View history shows every change, and Create reorder task adds a task for low items. Adding, editing, costing or deleting an ingredient itself is owner-only.

Subscribers

The Subscribers page lists everyone on a meal plan: their plan, what they picked for each meal, delivery details, the amount and whether it's paid.

  • Mark paid once you've received a subscriber's payment. A renewed cycle starts unpaid again.
  • Pause, Resume or Cancel (asks you to confirm) when a customer asks.
  • Each day, orders for active subscribers appear by themselves on the Orders board with a subscription badge. Cook them like any other order.

Creating plans, discounts and the delivery fee are owner-only.

Staff & tasks

You can see the full roster. You can change your own status (clocked in / on break / absent / clocked out). The dropdowns for other people are greyed out, because only the owner can change someone else's.

Task board: tick tasks done, edit them, or add a new one and assign it to a teammate.

Adding/removing staff, editing shift schedules and granting logins are owner-only actions.

My account

Open My account in the sidebar (or the ☰ menu on mobile) to change your own password.

  1. Enter your current password.
  2. Enter a new one, at least 8 characters, twice.
  3. Tap Update password.

A publicly known password such as the sample ones can't be used. If you forget it completely, ask your owner.

Notifications

The bell icon checks for alerts (like low stock) every 30 seconds. Turn on push notifications from the sidebar to hear about new orders even when the app is closed. Logging out asks you to confirm.

Need something you don't see here, like editing the menu, viewing reports, changing hours or delivery fees, or adding a teammate? That's your owner's job. Ask them.

Customer Guide

Browse the menu, order online, and track what you've ordered from Devi's Kitchen.

← Back to the app
  Browsing the menu doesn't need an account. To check out, create one (name, email, phone number, a password of at least 8 characters, and a 6-digit PIN) or log in if you already have one.

Order online

The main menu page, open to everyone, logged in or not.

  • Search the whole menu by name, or browse by category chip (Mains, Breads, Starters, Desserts, Beverages, Custom order, and any others the kitchen has added).
  • Today's Specials: a highlighted strip at the top, when the kitchen has anything featured that day. These clear automatically at midnight.
  • Tap the ♥ on any item to favorite it (saved on your device, not tied to your account).
  • Some items have a photo carousel. Swipe or use the arrows to see extra photos.
  • Custom order tab: for made-to-order items priced by weight (like "Biryani by the kg"). Enter the quantity in kg, optionally a protein/style, spice level, and a needed-by date. The price calculates as you type, and the restaurant always re-checks it when you order.
  • Your order panel (right side, or the bottom bar on mobile): everything in your cart with any notes, and the running total.
  • Each item shows how long it'll take right under the price: "Ready now" or "~N min".

Checking out

We cook fresh after you order. Nothing is pre-made, so items with a prep time will take a little longer.
  1. Add items to your cart.
  2. If you're not logged in, you'll see a Log in to place your order card instead of the checkout form. Your cart stays put while you log in or sign up.
  3. Choose how you're getting it: Delivery, Pickup or Dine-in (dine-in asks for a table number).
  4. For Delivery, pick a saved address or add a new one (type it, use your current location, drop a pin on the map, or paste a Google Maps link). Tick "save" to keep it for next time, and add a phone number for the rider.
  5. When do you want it? Choose As soon as possible (only while the restaurant is open) or Choose a time and pick a day and a time slot. If we're closed right now, ASAP is switched off and you book a slot instead.
  6. Pick a payment method: Cash, UPI or Card.
  7. Click Checkout.

Delivery fee. The fee depends on how far your address is from the restaurant, and appears under your items before you order. Some distances have a minimum order, and a bigger order can make delivery free. If your address is too far away, checkout tells you. If we couldn't pinpoint your address, you'll see "estimated": choose your location on the map to get the exact fee.

Every order starts as unpaid, whatever you choose. The restaurant marks it paid once they've confirmed the money. For Cash, you pay on delivery or pickup. For UPI or Card, see "Paying by UPI" below.

Once placed, you'll see a confirmation with a link to your receipt, and a live tracker follows your active order around the site until it's done.

Paying by UPI

For an unpaid UPI or Card order, your order shows a Pay ₹… via UPI card (when the restaurant has set up its UPI ID).

  1. Scan the QR code with any UPI app, or tap Pay with UPI app on your phone. The amount is filled in for you.
  2. After paying, tap Upload payment receipt and choose a screenshot of the confirmation.
  3. The restaurant checks it and marks your order paid. You can replace the screenshot if you uploaded the wrong one.

Your order isn't held up while it waits: the kitchen starts as normal.

My orders

Your full order history, newest first: items, type, when it's for, delivery fee, total, payment status and live status. It refreshes by itself every 30 seconds while an order is in progress.

  • Click receipt on any order to view or print it.
  • You can cancel an order yourself, but only while it's still "placed". Once the kitchen starts preparing it, please call the restaurant.

Meal plans (subscribe & save)

Open Meal Plans in the top bar. Instead of ordering each day, you pick what you'd like to eat and the restaurant cooks it for you every day of your plan. Anyone can look; you need an account to subscribe.

  1. Under Available plans, choose one. Each plan shows how long it runs (Daily, Weekly or Monthly), which meals it covers (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) and how many items you can pick per meal.
  2. Use the meal tabs to choose items for each meal. A ✓ marks what you've picked, and the counter (like 1/2) shows your limit. Tap an item again to remove it.
  3. Choose Delivery (with an address and optional phone) or Pickup, and a payment method.
  4. The Your total box updates as you pick: price per day, times the days in the plan, any discount for longer plans, and a delivery fee per day for delivery plans.
  5. Tap Subscribe & pay.

My subscriptions (at the top of the page once you have one) shows each plan, what you picked, the amount per cycle and whether it's paid. You can Pause, Resume or Cancel it yourself at any time. A paused plan gets no daily orders.

  • Every day the kitchen automatically gets an order for your picks, so you don't have to order. These appear under My orders like any other order.
  • When a cycle ends, the plan renews itself for another cycle at the current prices. You'll get a notification, and the new cycle shows as unpaid until the restaurant marks it paid.
  • Your payment shows as Pay in person until the restaurant confirms it.
To change what you've picked, cancel the plan and subscribe again with new items. Items and prices come from the menu, so a plan can only include regular, priced dishes that are in stock.

My account

Open My account from the top bar (or the bottom bar on mobile).

  • Profile: change your name and phone number. Your email can't be changed here.
  • Security: change your password, or your 6-digit PIN. Both need your current password first.
  • Saved addresses: add, edit, delete, and choose which one is your default for delivery.

Forgot your password?

On the log in page, choose Forgot your password?

  1. Enter your email, your 6-digit PIN and a new password (at least 8 characters).
  2. Forgotten your PIN too? Choose Request help from the store and enter your email. The restaurant is notified and will set a new PIN for you.
Too many wrong tries in a row will pause logging in for about 15 minutes. Wait a little and try again.
